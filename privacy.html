PropLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PropLens is a tool for analyzing rental-property numbers you enter yourself. It is built to keep your information on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what that means.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in PropLens is stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage:

  • Properties and their financial inputs
  • Input-change history, scenarios, Target Price and sensitivity analyses
  • Notes, due-diligence checklists
  • Documents and photos you attach
  • Reports you generate and your report-branding details (name, company, logo, colour)

We do not keep a copy of any of this on a server, because there is no server.

Nothing is sent to a server

PropLens works fully offline. It does not transmit your data anywhere. When you capture a listing from a screenshot, the reading of that image (text recognition and field extraction) happens entirely on your device — the screenshot, any shared text, and the extracted numbers never leave it.

No account, no sign-up

There is no login and no registration. PropLens does not ask for or collect your name, email, or any other contact information to use the app. A Pro subscription is linked to your Apple ID through the App Store, not to any PropLens account.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

PropLens contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no cross-app tracking. There is no advertising identifier (IDFA), no user profiling, and nothing that follows you across other apps or websites.

Subscriptions and payments

PropLens Pro is sold as an in-app subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We never see or receive your card number or payment details.

What we never collect

  • No accounts and no contact-information harvesting
  • No location data beyond the addresses you type into a property yourself
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Children

PropLens is rated 4+ and is a general-purpose finance tool. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the date above before that behaviour ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email support@proplens.app.
