PropLens — Support

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PropLens helps you analyze rental-property deals from numbers you enter. If something isn't working or you have a question, we're happy to help.

Contact us

support@proplens.app

For help with the app and for any privacy or data questions. We aim to reply within a couple of business days.

Your data is on your device

PropLens stores everything locally on your device and sends nothing to a server. Because of that, your properties, notes, documents and reports are included in your device's normal backup. If you delete the app, its data is removed with it.

Managing your subscription

PropLens Pro is billed by Apple through the App Store. To view, change or cancel it, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use Restore Purchases in Profile to recover an active subscription.

Exporting your data

You can export all of your properties as a CSV or JSON file from Profile → Data, then share or save the file wherever you like.

Reports and branding

Generate a PDF report from any property's "Report" option. Pro subscribers can set a name, company, logo and brand colour once in Profile → Report branding, and it is applied to every report.

Common questions

  • Does PropLens give investment advice? No — it shows estimates based on the inputs and assumptions you enter. It is not financial, legal, tax or investment advice.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and nothing leaves your device.
  • Which markets does it support? It uses US conventions (USD, US mortgage math) and is available in English.
